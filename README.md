# Find Cost of Tile to Cover W × H Floor

## Description

This C++ program calculates the total cost of tiles required to cover a rectangular floor.

The program takes the **floor width**, **floor height**, and **tile price per square foot** from the user. It then calculates the floor area and the total cost of the tiles.

## Formula

**Floor Area:**

`Area = Width × Height`

**Total Cost:**

`Total Cost = Area × Tile Price`

## Example

```text
Enter floor width: 10
You entered floor width: 10

Enter floor height: 12
You entered floor height: 12

Area of floor: 120 square feet

Enter tile price per square foot: 50

Total cost of tiles: 6000
```

1. Download or clone this repository.

5. The program will display the floor area and total cost.
